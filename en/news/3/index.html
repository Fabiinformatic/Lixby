The birth of LixBuds One

The idea for LixBuds One is born, our first wireless earbuds with extended battery life and premium sound. It's still in beta.

Lixby Newsroom · 25 february 2026 · Read: 4 min

At Lixby an idea was born today: LixBuds One, our first foray into the world of wireless audio. These are earbuds designed to accompany you all day, with a battery that aims to keep the music going and sound that puts quality first.

We want LixBuds One to combine immersive sound, noise cancellation and simple controls in a comfortable, discreet format. Its portable charging case is intended to ensure energy lasts where it's needed most, while each earbud is designed to fit naturally and not distract during continuous use.

An idea still in beta

LixBuds One's design philosophy starts from a simple idea: audio should be there when you need it, without complications. That's why the controls would be reduced to the essentials, the connection would be immediate and ergonomics would be cared for down to the smallest detail. But we haven't launched anything yet: the project is in beta, taking its first steps.

Noise cancellation aims to let you focus on what matters both in a noisy environment and in a moment of calm, and playback would maintain a balanced sound signature, designed to enjoy every music genre just as it was recorded.

A new chapter for Lixby

With LixBuds One, Lixby wants to expand its catalogue beyond software and enter hardware. It is the result of months of attentive listening to those who take their earbuds everywhere, on the way to work, at the gym or at home.

LixBuds One is still on its way. We'll keep reporting here, in the Lixby Newsroom, on how the idea progresses towards launch.
