The birth of Lixney

The idea for Lixney is born, our personal finance app. It's still in beta.

Lixby Newsroom · 4 February 2026 · Read: 4 min

At Lixby an idea was born today: Lixney, a personal finance app with a clear goal — that anyone can take control of their money without needing to be an accounting expert. The idea is presented with a simple, orderly proposal designed for daily use.

We want Lixney to help organise expenses, create budgets and better understand where money goes. Far from cold numbers, the app would turn information into a clear view, so that every financial decision is made with context and peace of mind.

An idea still in beta

The idea behind Lixney is to remove the usual complications of personal finance. Expenses would be classified automatically, budgets would adapt to real life and the history would show how things evolve over time. But we haven't launched anything yet: the project is in beta, taking its first steps.

The design would focus on the essentials: understanding the present to plan the future. With useful notifications, periodic summaries and intuitive navigation, Lixney aims to become a quiet companion that's there when you need it.

Friendly finances

We're designing Lixney with real people in mind, with different rhythms of life and changing priorities. That's why the app shouldn't judge or complicate: it should accompany, organise information and offer an honest view of each user's financial situation.

Lixney is still on its way. We'll keep reporting here, in the Lixby Newsroom, on how the idea progresses towards launch.
