The birth of Lixfly

The idea for Lixfly is born, our EFB for flight simulation in MSFS2020/2024. It's still in beta.

Lixby Newsroom · 1 april 2026 · Read: 4 min

At Lixby an idea was born today: Lixfly, designed for those who experience aviation through simulation. It is an Electronic Flight Bag (EFB) that would accompany the virtual pilot at every stage of the flight, from the pre-flight briefing to landing, without losing focus on immersion.

We want Lixfly to bring together in a single experience tools that until now force you to jump between several windows and applications. Preflight, flight plan, performance, navigation, eQRH and other utilities would be integrated into a clean panel, designed to be consulted naturally while flying MSFS2020 or MSFS2024.

An idea still in beta

The goal is clear from the start: to reduce the friction between planning and flying. But we haven't launched anything yet. Lixfly is in beta, taking its first steps, and little by little we'll polish every detail with the community's help before talking about any availability.

Integration with popular simulators aims to reflect plan data consistently throughout the session, so that every change to the route is reflected instantly and without constant manual maintenance.

Listening to the simulation community

We've started listening to the simulation community to understand what they miss. The ambition is an EFB that puts the essentials first: times, distances, fuel, weather and procedures, presented clearly and accessibly during any phase of flight.

With this birth, Lixby takes another step in its commitment to creating technology that helps you wherever you are, including within the world of flight simulation. We'll keep reporting here, in the Lixby Newsroom, on how Lixfly progresses on its path to launch.
