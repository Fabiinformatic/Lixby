Care and cleaning

Tips to keep your LixBuds and their case in perfect condition.

Maintenance

Take care of your LixBuds

1. Clean the earbuds

Use a soft, dry cloth. Remove earwax with a mesh cleaning tool.

2. Take care of the case

Clean the contacts with a cotton swab and avoid excessive moisture.

3. Store and charge properly

Keep your LixBuds in the case when not in use and avoid extreme temperatures.
