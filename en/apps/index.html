The Lixby philosophy

Less noise. More clarity.

We build apps that make the complex easier to understand, with honest interfaces and features that have a purpose.

Four principles that recur on every Lixney and Lixfly screen.

  1. 01Intuitive design
  2. 02Data under your control
  3. 03Continuous improvement
  4. 04Approachable support
01
01 — Design

Intuitive design

Every screen reads at a glance: fewer menus, fewer layers and fewer steps between you and the information you need.

One action per screenNo tutorialsReadable first time

In practice

  • A single main action per view, always in the same place.
  • No wizards or nested menus: everything is a couple of taps away.
  • High contrast and large type, also designed for a phone in your hand.
  • Empty states explain what to do, not just what's missing.
02
02 — Privacy

Data under your control

Your expenses and your flights are yours. Exporting them, deleting them or simply understanding what we store should be easy.

No adsOne-click exportReal deletion

In practice

  • You can export your data whenever you want and take it elsewhere.
  • Deleting your account also deletes stored information, not just access.
  • No advertising profiles and no selling data to third parties.
  • Local preferences whenever the app doesn't need a connection.
03
03 — Evolution

Continuous improvement

We release versions regularly and explain what changes. What doesn't add value stays out.

Release notesRoadmap in betaFast fixes

In practice

  • Every update comes with its release notes, with no small print.
  • What reaches the beta is decided with feedback from those who use it.
  • A clear notice when an improvement changes something you already used.
  • If a feature doesn't add value, it's removed: fewer options, less noise.
04
04 — Support

Approachable support

A real team member replies, with no endless tickets and no copied answers. People before software.

Real peopleNo botsBeta priority

In practice

  • You write to a team member, not a form nobody reads.
  • No bots or automatic replies: if it needs a personal answer, you get one.
  • Beta issues are handled first.
  • Documentation in the same language as the app.
