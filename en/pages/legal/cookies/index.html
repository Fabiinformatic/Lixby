What are cookies?

At Lixby (https://lixby.es) we use cookies and similar technologies for the correct operation of the site and, when you give us your consent, to analyse how the website is used and improve your experience.

A cookie is a small file stored on your device when you visit a website. It allows your browser to be recognised and certain information to be remembered between visits.

Data controller

Lixby S.L. ("Lixby", "we"), with tax ID [tax ID pending], website https://lixby.es. For any questions about this policy, contact us at info@lixby.es.

Managing your consent

When you visit Lixby for the first time, a banner lets you Accept or Reject analytics cookies. Your decision is stored on your device and you can change it at any time from the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

If you reject or disable cookies from your browser, some features of the site may be limited, although essential operation is not affected.

Third-party cookies

Some cookies are managed by third parties in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google (Analytics): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Cloudflare: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

How to disable cookies in your browser

In addition to our settings panel, you can block or delete cookies from your browser settings:

  • Chrome: https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/32050
  • Microsoft Edge: https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09
  • Firefox: https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/delete-cookies-remove-info-websites-stored
  • Safari: https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-and-website-data-sfri11471/mac

If you reject or disable cookies from your browser, some features of the site may be limited, although essential operation is not affected.
