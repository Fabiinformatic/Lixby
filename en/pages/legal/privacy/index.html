Lixby Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Lixby S.L. ("Lixby", "we"), with tax ID [tax ID pending], collects, uses and protects your personal data when you use lixby.es, Lixney or Lixfly (together, the "Service").

Data controller

Lixby S.L., [tax ID pending], Spain.

Contact email info@lixby.es

Data we collect

Account data

Name, email, password (encrypted), Lixby account number (format NLX-XXXXXXXX).

Purchase data

Shipping/billing address, order history. Payment data (card) is processed directly by Stripe — Lixby does not store card numbers.

Support data

Ticket content, email correspondence, support conversation history.

Usage data

IP address, browser type, pages visited, via Google Analytics (only if you accept analytics cookies — see our Cookie Policy).

Push notifications

If you use Lixney or Lixfly and enable notifications, we collect the device identifier necessary to send them to you. You can disable them at any time from the app or operating system settings.

Who we share your data with

We do not share your data with affiliates, parent companies or commercial partners — Lixby is a single entity and does not make such transfers. We only share data with the following providers, which act as data processors under contract:

ProviderPurposeLocation
Google (Firebase/Google Cloud)Authentication, database, hostingEU / USA*

*When a provider processes data outside the EEA, we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place (EU Standard Contractual Clauses or another valid mechanism under the GDPR).

We never sell your personal data to third parties under any circumstances.

Legal basis for processing

  • Performance of a contract: management of your account, orders and support.
  • Consent: analytics cookies, push notifications, commercial communications.
  • Legitimate interest: site security, fraud prevention.
  • Legal obligation: retention of tax/accounting data.

Data retention

  • User accounts: while the account is active, plus up to 24 months after closure.
  • Support tickets: up to 24 months from closure.
  • Billing data: according to legal tax periods (Spain: 6 years).
  • Analytics data: up to 24 months from collection.

Your rights

You can access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and request portability, by writing to info@lixby.es. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) if you consider that your rights have not been respected.

Minors

The Service is not aimed at children under 14 years of age (the minimum age for own consent in Spain under the GDPR and the LOPDGDD). We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age without parental consent.

Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures (encryption, authentication, access control) to protect your data, although no system is 100% secure.

Changes to this Policy

We will notify you of relevant changes by email or by a prominent notice in the Service, and we will update the "Last updated" date.
