Legal

Returns Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Right of withdrawal

If you buy as a consumer, you have the right to withdraw from your purchase within 14 calendar days of receiving the product, without giving a reason.

Legal exceptions: The right does not apply, among other cases provided by law, to personalised products, products sealed for hygiene reasons that have been unsealed, or digital content and services whose download or activation has begun with your consent.

2. Conditions for returns

Included products:

  • Physical products purchased as a consumer, except for the legal exceptions.

You may examine the product to check its nature, characteristics and operation. The law may hold you liable for any reduction in value resulting from handling beyond what is necessary for that check.

3. Return process

Step 1: Notification

Communicate your decision to withdraw within 14 calendar days of receipt by emailing info@lixby.es with your order number and an unequivocal statement that you wish to withdraw. You do not need to give a reason.

  • Order number
  • Contact details so we can reply to you

Step 2: Authorisation

You will receive a return authorisation (RMA) along with shipping instructions.

Step 3: Shipping

We will send you the instructions and return address once we confirm receipt of your request.

Step 4: Refund

The refund will be made using the same payment method and within the legal deadline. We may withhold it until we receive the product or until you provide proof of return, whichever happens first.

4. Shipping costs

Free initial shipping: Orders are shipped free of charge, with no minimum amount.

Return:

  • If the product is defective or the shipment was incorrect: Lixby pays the return costs.
  • If you exercise the right of withdrawal because you changed your mind: you pay the direct return costs, unless we have informed you otherwise.

5. Non-refundable products

The right of withdrawal does not apply in the following cases provided by law:

  • Personalised or configured products
  • Products sealed for hygiene reasons that have been unsealed after delivery
  • Digital content or services whose download or activation has begun with your express consent

6. Product exchanges

The legal right is one of withdrawal during the 14 calendar days. If you want another variant, you can give notice of withdrawal and place a new order.

7. Partial refunds

If the value of the product has decreased due to handling beyond what is necessary to check its nature, characteristics and operation, the legally provided liability may apply. We will inform you of the calculation before processing the refund.

8. Defective or damaged products

If the order arrives damaged in transit or contains an incorrect item, contact info@lixby.es as soon as possible and attach photos of the product and packaging to speed up the process. This notice does not limit the legal warranty for lack of conformity.

  1. State your order number and describe the problem.
  2. Lixby covers the return costs when the product is defective or the shipment was incorrect.
  3. We will inform you of the options available to resolve it.

9. Warranty policy

Physical products have a 3-year legal warranty from delivery for lack of conformity. This warranty is independent of the 14-day period for exercising the right of withdrawal.

For warranty claims, contact info@lixby.es.

10. Disputes and complaints

If you are not satisfied with the return or refund process, you can:

  • Contact our customer service department
  • Submit a formal complaint through our contact form
  • Use the dispute resolution mechanisms available in your country
