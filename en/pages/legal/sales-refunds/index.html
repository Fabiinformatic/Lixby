Sales and Refunds

Last updated: 23 August 2026

This page explains how returns, exchanges and refunds work for purchases made at https://lixby.es. It complements our Terms and Conditions.

Right of withdrawal (14 days)

If you are a consumer and buy from the European Union, you have the right to return your order within 14 calendar days of receiving it, without having to give a reason.

How to exercise it:

  • Write to info@lixby.es stating your order number and that you wish to withdraw from the purchase.
  • We will confirm receipt of your request and tell you how and where to send the product.
  • Send the product in its original packaging, unused, with all accessories included.

Refund period:

We refund the amount paid (including the initial shipping costs, if any) within a maximum of 14 days from receiving your notice of withdrawal or, if earlier, from receiving the returned product. The refund is made using the same payment method you used for the purchase.

Return costs:

These are at your expense, unless the product arrived faulty or we sent you the wrong item — in that case, we cover them.

Exceptions to the right of withdrawal

You cannot withdraw from:

  • Products you have unsealed for hygiene reasons (earbuds, if the hygiene seal has been broken).
  • Software licences or digital content once you have started downloading or using them with your express consent.
  • Personalised or custom-made products.

Faulty product or damage in transit

If the package arrives damaged during transport or contains the wrong item, contact us at info@lixby.es, preferably within 48 hours of receipt, with photos of the product and packaging to speed things up. This notice period does not reduce the 3-year legal warranty of conformity.

Legal warranty

Physical products (such as LixBuds One) have a legal warranty of conformity of 3 years from delivery, in accordance with Spanish and EU law. It covers manufacturing defects or lack of conformity with what was advertised; it does not cover damage from misuse, negligence, normal wear or unauthorised handling.

To make a warranty claim, contact info@lixby.es describing the problem and attaching proof of purchase.

Product exchanges

If you want to exchange your product for another variant (for example, another colour), treat it as a return within the 14-day period and place a new order for the item you prefer.

Cancelling orders before shipping

If your order has not yet been shipped, you can request its cancellation by writing to info@lixby.es; we will refund the full amount.

Contact

For any questions about returns or refunds: info@lixby.es

If we cannot reach a satisfactory solution, as an EU consumer you can use the online dispute resolution platform: https://ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr/
