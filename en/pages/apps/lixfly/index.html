Lixfly

Your EFB for MSFS2020 and MSFS2024.

Lixfly brings Preflight, Flight Plan, Performance, Navigation and eQRH together in a single app, designed so you fly with the data you need always at hand.

Help Center Want to be a Lixfly beta tester?

Main features

🛫 Preflight

Configure your flight in minutes: aircraft, route, airport version and fuel. Lixfly gets the aircraft ready for takeoff.

🗺️ Flight Plan

Enter origin, destination and altitude. Lixfly generates an optimised route with up-to-date NOTAMs and weather.

📊 Performance

Calculate fuel, weights and balance with Lixfly's built-in calculator, with results tailored to each aircraft.

🧭 Navigation

Maps, waypoints and in-flight navigation. Enable the external GPS and calibrate the compass for precise navigation.

📖 eQRH

The company operations manual always available, with quick search of procedures and checklists.
